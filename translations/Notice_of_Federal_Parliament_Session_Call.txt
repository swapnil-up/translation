Urgent Notice Regarding the Convening of the Session of the Federal Parliament

His Excellency the Right Honourable Ram Chandra Paudel, President of Nepal, has convened the session of the Federal Parliament in accordance with Article 93, Sub-article (1) of the Constitution of Nepal, at 2:00 PM on Monday, Baishakh 28, 2081 BS. Therefore, I hereby convene the session of the House of Representatives.

Details:

Place: House of Representatives Hall (Multipurpose Hall), Federal Parliament Building, Singha Durbar, Kathmandu.

Date: Monday, Baishakh 28, 2081 BS.

Time: 2:00 PM.
