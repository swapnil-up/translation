2083 Baisakh 25, Friday
Notice regarding the convening of a joint meeting
As per Article 94 of the Constitution of Nepal, the Right Honorable President Ram Chandra Poudel will address the joint meeting of both houses of the Federal Parliament on Monday, Baisakh 28, 2083, at 16:00 hrs, and for that purpose, members are requested to be present. This notice has been published for the information of the members of the Federal Parliament.
Furthermore, it is requested that all members of the House of Representatives be present in the House of Representatives (Multipurpose Hall, Singh Durbar) by 15:45 hrs on the said day and take their respective seats for the joint session meeting.
Padma Prasad Pandey
Secretary-General
